SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Burn Less Steam Making the Same Ethanol

How can industrial plants move beyond simply monitoring process data and instead use it to predict energy demand, understand their carbon impact, and identify smarter operating conditions — while maintaining production, product purity, and process recovery?

01 / THE PROBLEM

Increase in the Co2e and decrease in the Carbon Credit

Industrial operations consume huge amounts of energy and generate significant CO₂ emissions. Inefficient resource use increases environmental impact and makes it harder for industries to meet sustainability and decarbonization goals. Despite having vast operational data, many industries struggle to turn it into timely, measurable actions to reduce energy and carbon emissions.

02 / THE ARCHITECTURE

It Plugs Into What You Already Have

No rip and replace. We read your existing SCADA, PLC and Modbus registers, so the data you're already collecting starts earning its keep. The loop runs itself: Measure -> Understand -> Predict -> Optimize -> Recommend -> Validate, then round again with whatever changed.

SCADA/IoT -> ML Prediction -> Optimization -> LLM Advisory
03 / The Solution

Optimizing the CO2e

E-Optimizer transforms industrial process data into actionable energy and carbon intelligence. It uses machine learning to predict energy consumption, engineering models to quantify CO₂e emissions, and AI-driven analysis to identify lower-energy operating conditions. By evaluating these opportunities against production, purity, and recovery constraints, E-Optimizer helps industries reduce energy use, lower carbon emissions, and identify their potential for carbon reduction and credits.

Primary KPI: kg CO2e / L ethanol & MJ / L ethanol
PROCESS INTELLIGENCE

Critical Unit Operations Analyzed

DATA CHANNELS: 24V DC / RS485 / MODBUS TCP / MQTT
STAGE 01
Milling & Liquefaction
Grind too coarse and starch walks out with the DDGS. Grind too fine and you fight viscosity all the way through liquefaction. We read particle spread and slurry solids, then tell you the enzyme dose and hold time that convert the most starch for the least steam.
Viscosity vs Steam (kg/h)
STAGE 02
Fermentation Monitoring
A batch that sits two hours longer than it needs to is two hours of chilling you didn't have to pay for. We track yeast viability, pH drift and CO2 rate to spot when fermentation is genuinely finished, so you drop the tank on time and still hit your ABV.
Yield / Residence Time
STAGE 03
Distillation Optimization
This is where your steam goes. Most of it. We watch column temperatures, feed flow, reflux and bottom loss, then turn all of it into one number you can argue with: kilograms of steam per litre of pure ethanol.
Steam Intensity (kg steam / L)
STAGE 04
MEE & DDGS Drying
Overdrying DDGS means burning gas to remove water you were allowed to sell. We follow evaporator duty, fouling and exhaust temperature so you land on your moisture spec instead of sailing past it.
Thermal Duty / kg DDGS
OPTIMIZATION INTELLIGENCE

From Raw Telemetry to Explainable Recommendations

Underneath, three separate models handle prediction, optimization and carbon accounting. Your operator sees none of that. They get one recommendation and the reason behind it, which is the only part that helps at 3am.

04 / PREDICTIVE MODELS

Yield, Energy & Carbon Prediction

When energy climbs, the useful question isn't whether the plant is off. It's which stage. Models trained on your own operating history predict yield, specific energy and carbon intensity for any process state, so you get pointed at the column or the dryer instead of at the whole facility.

Gradient boosting, time-series forecasting, anomaly detection
05 / MULTI-OBJECTIVE OPTIMIZATION

Feasible Trade-offs, Not a Single Answer

Push yield up and energy follows it. Push energy down and recovery starts to slip. All three fight each other, so the engine searches inside your real constraints and hands back settings you can actually dial in, not an optimum that quietly breaks your purity spec.

Bounded by process, safety & quality constraints
06 / EXPLAINABLE ADVISORY

Ask The Plant Directly

Every recommendation shows its working: which variable moved, by how much, and what happened downstream. Ask why steam climbed this morning, or what another 5 t/day of grain would cost you in carbon intensity, and you get a sentence back. Not another chart to decode.

Natural-language queries, what-if simulation
VOICE INTERFACE

Ask It Out Loud, Gloves On

Nobody types on a walkway. The assistant takes spoken questions and answers back out loud, using the speech engine already built into the browser. No handset, no licence, no extra box on the plant network.

A background listener sits on the wake phrase and nothing else. 08 / SPOKEN QUESTIONS

Your Words Land In The Box

Press the microphone and the question appears as you say it, then sends itself the moment you stop talking. Same question, same answer, same refusal to invent a figure it cannot see. Voice is another way in, not a second assistant with looser standards.

Live transcript -> auto-submit -> same advisory engine
09 / SPOKEN ANSWERS

Units That Survive Being Read

Replies are read back automatically, with the units expanded first. 412.8 kg CO2e/kL comes out as "412.8 kilograms of C O 2 equivalent per kilolitre", not spelled letter by letter with a slash in the middle of it. One button stops it mid-sentence when you've heard enough.

Automatic playback, interruptible, errors stay unspoken
