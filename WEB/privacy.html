LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Industrial Privacy & Telemetry Policy

Your process data is commercially sensitive and we treat it that way. Here's plainly what we collect, where it sits, who can reach it, and what we will never do with it.

01 / TELEMETRY DATA OWNERSHIP

Facility Process Data Stays With The Plant

All raw sensor signals (temperatures, pressures, flow rates, slurry solids, enzyme dosages) and SCADA/PLC registers ingested through Modbus or MQTT remain the exclusive intellectual property of the operating plant. E-Optimizer does not resell, aggregate, or expose plant-specific performance metrics to third parties.

02 / HARDWARE & NETWORK ISOLATION

Hardware-Enforced Network Segregation

Connection between the industrial IoT gateway (ESP32 / Industrial Gateway) and existing plant control systems operates over read-only Modbus RTU/TCP boundaries where configured. E-Optimizer operates as an advisory layer and never overrides safety interlocks, emergency relief loops, or critical valve setpoints.

03 / CARBON ACCOUNTING INTEGRITY

Auditable Emission Factor Logs

Calculated greenhouse gas intensities (kg CO2e / L ethanol) and energy efficiency indices (MJ / L ethanol) are stored in immutable time-series data stores (PostgreSQL + TimescaleDB). All calculations reference standardized emission factors to support verified carbon accounting, clean fuel standard compliance, and environmental auditing.

04 / TERMS OF SERVICE & ADVISORY SCOPE

Advisory Nature of Machine Learning Models

Recommendations generated by E-Optimizer machine learning algorithms and LLM advisory layers are intended to support licensed plant operators and process engineers in identifying inefficiencies. Final operational execution remains under the discretion of qualified plant personnel.
